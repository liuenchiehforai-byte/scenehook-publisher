Privacy Policy for SceneHook Publisher

Effective date: 18 November 2026
Last updated: 18 November 2026
Meta App ID: 1370897371786664

1. Who we are

SceneHook Publisher (the “App”) is a private, internal publishing tool used only by its owner (the “Owner”) to publish and manage the Owner’s own content on Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts that the Owner administers. It is not a public product, a consumer app, or a service offered to other businesses or the general public.

This policy explains what data the App processes, how that data is used, and how someone can ask for it to be deleted. It applies to Platform Data obtained from Meta (including Facebook and Instagram) and to related records stored in connection with the App.

2. What the App does

The App is used to:

  • Publish, schedule, and check the status of posts and Reels on the Owner’s own Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts;
  • Read post metadata, publishing status, and available insights or metrics for those accounts;
  • Read comments on the Owner’s Instagram media and, where the Owner has enabled this feature, send automatic Instagram Direct replies to people who comment.

The App does not sell access to other people’s Pages or accounts, and it is not intended to be used by anyone who does not have a role on the App.

3. Data the App processes

Depending on how it is used, the App may process the following:

  • Account and authentication data. Facebook Page IDs, Instagram professional account IDs, Page and account names, and access tokens (including Page access tokens) needed to call Meta’s APIs on the Owner’s behalf.
  • Content the Owner creates. Videos, images, captions, titles, thumbnails, scheduling times, and other metadata for posts the Owner publishes or plans to publish.
  • Post and account metadata. Post IDs, permalinks, publishing and processing status, and similar records returned by Meta’s APIs.
  • Insights. Available performance metrics for the Owner’s posts and accounts, such as views or reach, when those metrics are retrieved to check that publishing worked.
  • Comments and related messaging (Instagram auto-reply). When auto-reply is enabled, the App may process a commenter’s Instagram username or user ID, the text of their comment on the Owner’s media, and the content of any automatic Direct message sent in response, together with timestamps and message or comment IDs needed to avoid duplicate replies.

The App does not intentionally collect government ID numbers, payment card data, precise location, or special-category personal data. Commenters should avoid including sensitive information in public comments.

4. How the data is used

Data is processed only for these purposes:

  • To authenticate to Meta’s APIs and publish or schedule the Owner’s content on the Owner’s own accounts;
  • To confirm that posts were created, published, or scheduled as intended, and to review basic performance;
  • To read comments on the Owner’s Instagram media and send an automatic Direct reply when that feature is enabled;
  • To maintain local logs needed to operate, debug, and keep the App secure;
  • To respond to a data-deletion or other privacy request, or to comply with applicable law.

The App does not use this data to build profiles of the general public, to show ads, or to train public AI models.

5. Where data is stored

Access tokens, configuration, and related records are stored on the Owner’s private machines and other systems under the Owner’s control. They are not stored in a public app backend provided to other customers, because the App has no public users.

Content that the Owner publishes, and comments or messages sent through Facebook or Instagram, is also stored by Meta on Meta’s systems in accordance with Meta’s terms and policies.

6. Sharing and sale of data

The Owner does not sell personal data, and does not share Platform Data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

Data may be disclosed only:

  • To Meta, as needed to operate Facebook and Instagram API features the Owner has authorized (for example, publishing a Reel or sending an Instagram Direct reply);
  • To a service provider acting solely on the Owner’s instructions, if one is later used to host this policy or operate the App, and only as allowed by Meta’s Platform Terms;
  • If required by law, a valid legal process, or to protect the Owner’s or another person’s legal rights.

7. Retention

Data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above: for example, tokens for as long as the App remains authorized to publish; post and scheduling records for operational history; and comment or auto-reply records for as long as needed to operate that feature and prevent duplicate replies. Data is deleted or irreversibly de-identified when it is no longer needed, when a valid deletion request is completed, or when the App’s authorization is removed, except where a limited record must be retained to comply with law or to complete a request.

8. How to request deletion of your data

Anyone whose data the App has processed can ask for that data to be deleted. This includes the Owner and people who have commented on the Owner’s Instagram media or received an automatic Direct reply.

To make a request:

  1. Send an email to liu.enchieh.forai@gmail.com with the subject line “Data deletion request — SceneHook Publisher”.
  2. Include your name, the Facebook or Instagram username or profile URL involved if you have one, a description of the data you want deleted, and enough detail to locate it (for example, the date of a comment or the post it was left on).
  3. The Owner will review the request, delete any matching data stored in connection with the App, and reply to confirm what was done, or explain if some data cannot be deleted (for example, content that exists only in your or the Owner’s Facebook or Instagram account, which must be managed in those products).

If you previously authorized the App through Facebook, you can also remove it and request deletion of your data from Meta’s side by going to Facebook Settings > Apps and Websites, removing SceneHook Publisher, and sending a data deletion request from the removed-apps list. If a data-deletion callback is configured for the App, Meta may notify the Owner automatically when you do this.

This process does not delete content you posted on Facebook or Instagram under your own account. To delete that content, use Facebook or Instagram’s own tools.

9. Access, correction, and other requests

You may use the same contact address to ask what data the App holds about you, to correct inaccurate data, or to ask questions about this policy. The Owner will respond in a reasonable time and may need to verify that the request is from the person concerned.

10. Children

The App is an internal tool for the Owner and is not directed at children. It is not intended to be used by anyone under 18.
